The same code the FM-1 runs, compiled for the browser: the engine, the screen and the controls. An audio file or your microphone stands in for Bluetooth and USB audio in.

FM-1Right-click a button to hold it · again to let goVISCERA
KNOBSTap REC to capture the input, tap again to end it. KNOB 1–4 work it like putty; hold REC and turn one for a robot; hold OCT− or OCT+ and turn to move the pair.

Live input (Bluetooth / USB)

A catches it: tap REC on HANDS, tap again. B catches what A plays.

Sample bank

Drop a WAV or MP3 on a slot, or click one to choose a file. A track set to REEL plays them (REEL S1–S16), as well as captures you KEEP.

Controls

Turning
drag up and down, scroll, or focus and use the arrow keys
REC
on HANDS, SHARDS, SPECTRA or SOURCE: tap to capture, tap to end; hold to overdub; hold and turn a knob for a robot; REC + PLAY clears; SAVE + REC keeps it on the reel
HOME
HANDS: stretch, pitch, position and size of the window, A and B together
FX
SHARDS: glass-shard grains, by shape, density, spray and trigger
SEL
SPECTRA: the phase vocoder; stretch, blur, shift, cross with the partner
EDIT
SOURCE (capture, reel or thru; input, rate, bounce), then MIX
ENV
COLOR: drive, comp, crush, tilt, noise
LFO
the matrix and the four modulators; hold LFO and turn a knob to map
GLO
SPACE, then DISSECT (the listener), then SETTINGS
ARP
perform: eight macros, scenes (ARP + pad)
SEQ
steps (SLICE, PITCH, LEN, PROB each) or CAROM
SAVE
projects, then sounds: REC loads or saves, SAVE + PLAY saves over; hold SAVE and turn a knob for MIDI learn
OCT− / OCT+
held with a knob: both tracks of the pair move, together or apart; both held: TEMP
SELECT · ALGORITHM · PRESETS
the page's views · the track (A–D) · the page's list
Pads
click, or type Z X C V… / and Q W E R T Y; sharps on S D F H J… and 3 4 6
Space
PLAY
MIDI
channels 1–4 play A–D; CCs, clock and start/stop follow
