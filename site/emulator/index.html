The same code the FM-1 runs, compiled for the browser: the engine, the screen and the controls. An audio file or your microphone stands in for Bluetooth and USB audio in.

FM-1Right-click a button to hold it · again to let goVISCERA
KNOBSKNOB 1–4 turn the four parameters on screen. SELECT pages through them, ALGORITHM picks the track, PRESETS picks the device or list.

Live input (Bluetooth / USB)

T2 (LOOP) and T4 (THRU) listen to it. Press REC on T2 to catch a loop.

Sample bank

Drop a WAV or MP3 on a slot, or click one to choose a file. Slots 1–5 start with sounds made here.

Controls

Turning
drag up and down, scroll, or focus and use the arrow keys
KNOB 1–4
the four parameters on screen
SELECT
the next four parameters (pages)
ALGORITHM
track 1–4
PRESETS
the device in this slot, a modulator's type, a route, a step or a slot
MASTER
output level
EDIT FX SEL ENV
material, grain, filter, color
GLO
space, then settings (Bluetooth, clock, panel, MIDI learn)
LFO
the four modulators and the matrix; hold LFO and turn a knob to map
ARP
perform: eight macros, scenes (ARP + pad)
HOME
mix: level, pan, send and filter for each track
REC
the page's action: record, lock, freeze, trigger
SAVE
projects, then sounds: REC loads or saves, SAVE + PLAY saves over; hold SAVE and turn a knob for MIDI learn
OCT− + OCT+
held together: TEMP, everything snaps back
Pads
click, or type Z X C V… / and Q W E R T Y; sharps on S D F H J… and 3 4 6
Space
PLAY
MIDI
channels 1–4 play tracks 1–4; CCs, clock and start/stop follow
